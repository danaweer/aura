# Normal / Blueprint presentation

Use **Normal / Blueprint** beside the viewer's 3D/Top view controls, including in fullscreen. Switching changes presentation only: camera position, target, zoom/FOV, floor, cutaway, selected room/asset and all equipment/service/device filters remain intact. Blueprint retains the current model coordinates and IDs; it adds no equipment, routes or device quantities.

The navy background and subdued world grid frame white architectural crease outlines. `EdgesGeometry` uses a 35° threshold rather than triangle wireframes. Fine world-space hatching is limited to selected architectural surfaces; furniture/site detail becomes faint. Equipment, safety signs and extinguishers retain their geometry and recognisable colours/textures. R01 route colours populate the compact legend, and selections use a gold bounding outline and static asset label. Existing room and plan labels stay available through their existing controls.

`blueprint.js` lazily caches presentation materials and edge geometry. Instanced edge batches copy the model's instance matrices; their bounds retain actual transformed extents so equipment camera fitting is unchanged. Overlays inherit their source object's floor, layer and transforms and have no raycast handler. The original pickable model remains authoritative. Repeated switching reuses the cache, and `dispose()` removes overlays and releases only owned resources.

During Blueprint drawing, materials are temporarily substituted and restored in `finally`, including after a renderer error. Visibility rules and picking therefore continue to see original shared materials. Normal restores the original background, fog, environment and materials exactly; no camera/view setters run during switching. Lighting and shadow settings are restored after drawing.

P01 equipment, R01 routes and D02 room-device candidates remain conceptual, including tank-space reservations and pending fire/atrium studies. The visual mode does not resolve sizes, coverage, quantities, equipment selection or engineering. Unconnected building assets say **Not connected — integration planned.** No IoT bindings, readings, polling or protocols were added; the existing meeting-room project remains separate.

Checks cover original-material restoration (including a failed draw), instanced matrices and equipment bounds, non-pickable overlays, floor/service filtering, visible-only selection highlights, repeated switching and resource disposal. The browser regression checks verify camera/layer/selection preservation, exact Normal framebuffer restoration, physical device picking, fullscreen and no WebGL/shader console errors. They also retain existing model and meeting-room telemetry checks.
