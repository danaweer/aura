# AURA model and platform audit — 30 October 2026

Reviewed the Three.js building, geometry/material/visibility helpers, room and asset inspection, project navigation, fullscreen controls, conceptual services, Python HTTP/SSE bridge, device decoding and history handling. The meeting-room device scope remains separate from the building.

## Issues corrected

| Finding | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| Washroom detail could remain active when another room was selected on the same floor, leaving partitions in the wrong cutaway state. | Room and general asset selection now exit washroom detail and reapply visibility. The dedicated detail button still restores the intended inspection view. |
| A previously selected room outline remained highlighted while inspecting an exit sign, extinguisher or BMS point. | Asset inspection clears and disposes the obsolete outline. The last room choice remains available for route tracing. |
| Returning from another project rebuilt the room selector without restoring the viewer's room context. | The selector now follows the viewer's retained room ID, keeping tracing controls consistent after project switches. |

| Plumbing floor/roof datums duplicated fixed numbers. | Pipe stacks and vent heights now derive from the existing envelope/floor configuration. |
| Project descriptions lagged behind the extinguisher and washroom work; a single accessible WC was labelled “1 WCs”. | The catalog describes 27 FE/SG stations, nine washrooms, conceptual piping and 84 unbound BMS/fan locations; singular/plural display is corrected. ## Remaining design and validation work

The 40 × 24 m source envelope is still a schematic assumption, not a survey. Service equipment, pipe/duct sizes, gradients, waterproofing, penetrations, supports, accessibility, egress/public-way strategy and extinguisher coverage require discipline review. Local geometry checks do not constitute a complete multidisciplinary clash or compliance assessment.

The 84 washroom/fan locations are model-only and unavailable. No live building BMS or assistance-alarm hardware was tested or commissioned. The platform tests exercise simulated/fixture telemetry; they do not verify an actual MXCHIP on the user's network. Real device binding and commissioned state/proof/fault handling remain a subsequent integration stage.

Further performance work should be guided by measurements on target desktop/mobile hardware. Instancing leaves the scene's triangles and intended appearance intact; it is not a claim of a specific frame rate.
