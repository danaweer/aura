        Projects and the 3D workspace

        Use Projects to switch between your preserved meeting room and the three-floor atrium building. The building includes your office layouts and furniture. Office cutaway and Top view reveal each floor; room labels and Find a room show room IDs and areas. Service reserves reveal empty plant rooms, aligned risers, and overhead route allowances. Finished exterior, front, rear, side and roof inspection views are available. Roofs and Ceilings are separate controls and automatically hide during floor isolation. Full screen opens the building with a visibility sidebar; Hide controls gives more canvas space. Press Esc or Exit full screen to return. Left-drag orbits, middle-drag pans, and the wheel zooms. Concept services toggles rooftop placeholders and connected air paths: blue supply, sage return and amber sanitary exhaust. This is a conceptual model, not an installation design.

        The MXCHIP AZ3166 supplies real sensor values through its Modbus TCP Server on your Wi-Fi network. The Python application reads the device every second and pushes readings to this page using Server-Sent Events (SSE).

        Drag the room to orbit. Scroll to zoom. With the room canvas focused, use arrow keys to orbit, +/− to zoom, and Home to reset. Click sensor tags to inspect assets. Press physical Button A to toggle your firmware's demo alarm (Modbus input register 30006); the room and dashboard will respond after the next successful poll.

        Other room fixtures are schematic and are not connected to field equipment. Start the server using --demo to explore using clearly labeled simulated telemetry when your MXCHIP is unavailable.
